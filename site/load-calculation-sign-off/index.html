The problem

You can pull the permit. You can do the work. You can't sign the calculation.

That's the wall most people doing their own electrical work hit — and it's usually the last thing standing between them and an approved permit.

Why this exists

Homeowners can take out a homeowner electrical permit and do the installation themselves. But the City generally will not accept a load calculation prepared by the homeowner — it needs to be completed and signed by a licensed or master electrician. So people do all the work, then discover they need a signature they can't provide.

That's the whole job here. Not a service call, not a quote for work you're already doing yourself — just the calculation, done properly, signed, and handed to you ready to submit.

Common questions

Load calculation sign-off FAQ

Can I do my own load calculation as a homeowner?

You can pull a homeowner electrical permit and do the installation work yourself, but the load calculation generally has to be prepared and signed by a licensed or master electrician before the permit is approved. That signature is what this service provides.

Do you have to come to my house?

Usually not. Most residential load calculations can be done over a video call where you walk through the panel, service and major appliances while I build the calculation live. In-person visits are available in the Calgary area when a project warrants one.

What do I get at the end?

A completed CEC Rule 8-200 load calculation showing every demand factor, the calculated amps, service OCP and conductor sizing — plus the completed City of Calgary load calculation form, signed and ready to submit.

How long does it take?

The walkthrough is typically under an hour for a standard house. You get the signed calculation and city form after the call.

What if the calculation shows my service is too small?

You get a straight answer and your options: change the equipment specification, add load management such as an EVEMS if EV charging is the driver, or upgrade the service. Finding out now is far cheaper than finding out at rough-in. See can a 100 A service handle a secondary suite for the detail.

What does it cost?

Online sign-offs start at $120 and in-home visits in the Calgary area start at $250. That covers the calculation, the completed City of Calgary form, and the sign-off. Most standard homes land at the starting price; anything more involved is quoted before you book, not after.

Which areas do you cover?

Calgary and the surrounding area. Online sign-offs are done by video, so there's no travel time and appointments are usually available sooner. In-person visits are available across the Calgary area.
